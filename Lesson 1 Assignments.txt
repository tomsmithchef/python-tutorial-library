Introduction

In this assignment, you will write a Python program that creates two sets: one for students on the baseball team and another for students on the basketball team. Perform various set operations to analyze the membership of the students in these sports teams.

Instructions

Complete the following steps:

Open PyCharm IDE.
Create a new Python file and name it {name}SportsTeams.py.
Write a Python program that creates two sets: one for students on the baseball team and another for students on the basketball team. Perform various set operations to analyze the membership of the students in these sports teams.
Run your code to ensure it correctly performs the set operations.
Save your Python file.
Teams:

Basketball team: Jack, Jess, Jane, Jen, Juliet, Jap
Baseball team: Jack, Jane, Juliet, Jacob, Jax, Justice
Operations to Perform:

Find the intersection of the sets to display the names of students who play both sports.
Find the union of the sets to display the names of students who play either sport.
Find the difference of the baseball and basketball sets to display the names of students who play baseball but not basketball.
Find the difference of the basketball and baseball sets to display the names of students who play basketball but not baseball.
Find the symmetric difference of the basketball and baseball sets to display the names of students who play one sport but not both.
